What we store

Account email and display name, hashed passwords (never plaintext), API token hashes (never the secret), login sessions (IP and user-agent), and per-request usage metadata (endpoint, status, latency, timestamp) retained for a limited window.

Why

To operate the service, authenticate requests, enforce rate limits, secure accounts, and show you your own usage.

Your rights

You can export all of your data or permanently delete your account at any time from the developer portal (Account → Your data).
