What a listing actually costs

Posting to the marketplace charges a fee up front. Put in what you plan to ask and see the fee, what you bank if it sells, and what you are out if nobody buys.

flux
Jump to

The fee across the price range

The fee climbs steeply near the top end and caps at 65,000 flux. As a share of the price it is cheapest in the middle of the market.

How the fee and tax work

You pay when you post
The fee comes out of your flux the moment the listing goes up, before anyone has bought anything.
It sells
You get the fee back and pay a 10% tax on the sale, so you keep 90% of the asking price.
Nobody buys it
No tax, but the fee is not returned. Overpricing a big listing is what actually costs you flux.

Why a pouch counts as two different numbers

250 credits
What you get when you open one. Use this to work out what the credits you already own are worth.
308.33 credits
What a pouch really costs you. The $99.99 pack pays out 18,500 credits but only 60 pouches, so each one sets you back more than its face value.
Which to pick
Valuing what you hold, use 250. Deciding whether buying is worth it, use 308.33. The two disagree by about a quarter, so it is worth getting right.
