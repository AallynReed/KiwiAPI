Why the Mods Hub?

A home for Trove mods that's quick for players and powerful for the people who make them. Here's what you get.

Find it, preview it, play it.

Available the moment it's out

New mods are live as soon as they're published - find it, download it, done. No waiting around.

See it in 3D first

Spin a mod's models in 3D right in your browser before you download a thing.

Watch creatures come alive

Creature mods can be viewed fully assembled on the in-game skeleton - posed and animated.

Drops in and just works

Downloads come named exactly how Trove expects, so they install and work the first time.

Whole sets in one go

Modpacks bundle a curated collection of mods into a single download.

Free to browse and grab

Browsing and downloading is completely free - no account needed. Sign in only to publish or save favourites.

A real home for your work.

Develop in the open

Full version history and releases keep your work organized - and nothing is ever lost.

Publish your way

Ship a polished, ready-to-play file built for you, or upload your own. Your call.

Offer variants

Ship a Full and a Lite edition side by side from one project, no juggling uploads.

Your own profile

A customizable page for everything you've made - avatar, banner, bio and links.

Credit that sticks

Forks and mods made in your honour always point back to the original work.

Build as a team

Add co-owners so a whole crew can maintain a mod together.

Your work, protected

Every release is fingerprinted, so nobody can re-upload your mod as their own.

Helpful by default

The studio catches misplaced files and fixes them in a click, so a mod is ready before you publish.

Built into Better Trove Tools

It all connects to the app, too - install mods, catch updates, and manage what you've got without ever leaving the toolkit.
