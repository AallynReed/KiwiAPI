What your tomes are worth

Tomes fill as you clear dungeons and pay out a fixed bundle of items. Here is what that bundle sells for right now, so you know which ones are worth your runs.

Regular tomes

No limit on these - fill the same one as often as you like. The top of this list is the best flux per dungeon run you can get from a tome.

Legendary tomes

One of each per week. Tick them off as you go - the list is yours alone and stays in this browser.

Tomes we cannot put a price on

These pay out in things that cannot be sold, so there is no market price to read. They are not worthless - we just have no honest way to value them.

Prices are the median of active marketplace listings, refreshed by the hourly scrape. A tome only shows a total when every item it gives has a price - a partial total would make a tome look cheaper than it is.
