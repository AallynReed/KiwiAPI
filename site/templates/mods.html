About Trove mods

Trove mods (.tmod files) let you reskin blocks, gear, mounts, allies, the interface and more - without permanently changing your game files. Browse the hub, download any mod and drop it into your Trove mods folder to try it out.

Want to share your own? Sign in to publish a mod with full version history and one-click releases, or bundle several into a modpack. Everything here is made by the community and free to use - see the full list on the site index.
