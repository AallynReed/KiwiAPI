How is this computed?

Three independent statistical checks per board: Modified Z-score (median-based, robust to cheaters polluting their own baseline), rank-gap ratio (a player's lead over the next rank vs the board's typical between-rank step), and velocity (score gain rate vs the board's peer p95). A player flagged by multiple checks, or across multiple boards, is higher confidence.

Captures come from an in-game scraping bot every hour. Boards reset on the same cadence Trove does (daily 11:00 UTC; weekly contests roll Mondays).
