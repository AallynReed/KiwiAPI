PvP doesn't use your character sheet as it is. Every stat is squeezed into a much smaller range, so a fresh character can still hold its own and better gear still wins, just by a smaller margin. Here is exactly how each stat converts in every PvP mode.

How it works

The game takes the number on your character sheet and runs it through up to four steps:

  1. A minimum. Most stats have a starting point everyone is raised to. A low stat simply becomes that number.
  2. A slowdown. Past that point, your stat grows with the square root of what you have. Four times as much over the line only doubles your bonus.
  3. Your role. In PvP, your class's role multiplies a few stats up or down. See Roles.
  4. A cap. Above the cap, growth slows again and creeps toward a ceiling it never quite reaches. In Bloodstone the cap is a hard stop instead.
if value < knee:  value = knee                    (for stats with a minimum)
else:             value = knee + k × √(value − knee)
value = value × role multipliers   (PvP only)
if value > cap:   value = cap + (ceiling − cap) × (1 − e^(−(value − cap) / (ceiling − cap)))

The knee, k, cap and ceiling are set per stat and per mode. The tables below list them in character-sheet units.

Roles

Every class has a PvP role. The role raises some stats and lowers others, after the minimum and before the cap, so a big multiplier still can't push a stat past its ceiling. Roles only apply in PvP: Battle Royale and Bloodstone ignore them.

What it means for your build

{% set hp = rule("pvp", "MaxHealth") %} {% set ms = rule("pvp", "MovementSpeed") %}

Everyone starts level

Below the minimum, gear makes no difference. A character with 150 health and one with {{ hp.min }} both start from {{ hp.min }}, before their role adjusts it.

Big leads shrink

Past the knee, a lot more stat buys a little more power. Going from 30,000 to 300,000 health, ten times as much, only takes you from {{ ex("pvp", "MaxHealth", 30000) }} to {{ ex("pvp", "MaxHealth", 300000) }} in PvP. Five times the Physical Damage, 10,000 against 50,000, is {{ ex("pvp", "PhysicalDamage", 10000) }} against {{ ex("pvp", "PhysicalDamage", 50000) }}.

Some caps are close, some are out of reach

Each stat has its own curve. Movement Speed reaches its cap of {{ ms.cap }} at about {{ ms.needed }} on your sheet, while Maximum Health would need {{ hp.needed }} to reach {{ hp.cap }}. Once a stat is near its cap, more of it does almost nothing, so check the tables before stacking one.

Bloodstone rewards gear more

Bloodstone's curves are much steeper and its caps are hard stops you can reach. The same 10,000 against 50,000 Physical Damage is {{ ex("bloodstone", "PhysicalDamage", 10000) }} against {{ ex("bloodstone", "PhysicalDamage", 50000) }} there, so the gap between characters stays wider.

Where this comes from

The curve was read out of the Trove game client (the 22 September 2026 build), and the numbers come from the game's PvP stat files, reread after every patch. The client's files have slots for per-class and per-role adjustments, but they ship empty: the game's servers send the role numbers, so the ones listed here come from the Trove team. See How Stat Modifiers Work for how the sheet value itself is built.
