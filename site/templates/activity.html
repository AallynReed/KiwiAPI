How this works: each capture counts the distinct top-leaderboard players whose score rose on at least one board since the previous capture, or who newly appear with a non-zero score - a lower bound on real active players (anyone outside every board's top ranks isn't seen). A reset isn't counted as activity (the score drops, it doesn't rise). The 24h and 7d figures are the distinct union of every capture's active players across the period. The chart plots the average per-hour estimate inside each time bucket.
