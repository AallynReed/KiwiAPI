Activity is estimated from each class's leaderboard, so it reflects active, higher-ranked players rather than a full headcount. The chart tracks each class's share of activity over time; the donut shows the split right now. "Established" hides brand-new characters and throwaway alts - "All" counts everyone. A player who plays several classes counts toward each, so the shares add up to 100% but aren't unique players.
